| What | Where | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Code**: scripts, tooling, site, packages, CI, docs | `tools/`, `site/`, `packages/`, `.github/`, `docs/`, `*.md` | [MIT](LICENSE) |
| **Data**: the database itself | `data/`, `schema/` | [CC0 1.0 Universal](LICENSE-DATA) (public domain dedication) |

## Why CC0 for the data

The goal is for distributions, migration tools and websites to use this data
without friction. CC0 means no attribution requirement and no share-alike
obligation, so anyone can embed, remix or republish it.

## What this means for contributors

By submitting a pull request you agree that:

- code you contribute is released under the MIT license, and
- data you contribute (anything under `data/` or `schema/`) is dedicated to the
  public domain under CC0 1.0.

Only contribute facts you verified yourself or can link to a source. Do not
copy text from other databases (WineHQ AppDB, AlternativeTo, ProtonDB, vendor
marketing pages) verbatim. Facts are free to record; their original wording is
not necessarily ours to republish.
