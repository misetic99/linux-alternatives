# Code of Conduct

This project is for people who are one app away from switching to Linux, and for people who help them get there. Everyone is welcome.

## Our standard

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good faith. Disagree about facts and data, not about people.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and spam.
- No advertising, no vendor or distro flame wars, no links to pirated software.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or PRs, and block contributors who violate this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the contact method listed on the repository's GitHub profile page, or by opening a confidential security/conduct advisory if the repository has enabled it.

This Code of Conduct is a short summary in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which maintainers may adopt in full as the project grows.
